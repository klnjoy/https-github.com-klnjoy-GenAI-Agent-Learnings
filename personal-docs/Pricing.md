## FAQ

??? question "Is there really a free tier?"
    Yes. All the question banks, model answers, self-quizzes, and the self-scored
    practice mode are free to use right now. Paid plans add AI grading, progress
    tracking, and downloadable summaries.

??? question "What does 'AI answer grading' do?"
    On Pro, you type an answer and get an AI-generated score plus specific
    feedback on what was strong and what to add — like a coach reviewing your
    response. (This runs against a reference answer for each question.)

??? question "When do paid plans launch?"
    Soon. The pricing here is a preview; checkout isn't live yet, so the Pro
    buttons are placeholders. Use the free tier in the meantime.

??? question "Can I cancel anytime?"
    Yes — monthly plans can be cancelled anytime and annual plans renew yearly.
    (Full terms will be published with launch.)

??? question "Do you offer student or team pricing?"
    That's planned. If you're interested in a team or student plan, that option
    will be added at launch.
