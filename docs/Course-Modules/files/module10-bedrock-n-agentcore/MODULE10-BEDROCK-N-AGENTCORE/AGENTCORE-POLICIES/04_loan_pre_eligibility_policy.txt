Document Title: Personal Loan Pre-Eligibility Policy
Document Type: Banking Policy
Department: Lending Operations
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-07-01

Purpose
This document defines preliminary eligibility checks for personal loan requests. Pre-eligibility is not final loan approval.

Policy Overview
Loan pre-eligibility can be estimated using customer profile attributes such as salary, credit score, relationship years, KYC status, risk segment, and requested loan amount.

Pre-Eligibility Criteria
A customer may be considered pre-eligible for loan review if:
- KYC status is verified.
- Risk segment is low or medium.
- Credit score is 700 or above.
- Customer has stable salary information.
- Requested amount is within the bank's preliminary affordability threshold.
- Customer has acceptable relationship history with the bank.

Example Rule
For demo purposes, a customer may pass pre-check if:
- KYC status is verified.
- Risk segment is low or medium.
- Credit score is at least 700.
- Requested loan amount is less than or equal to 15 times monthly salary.

Agent Allowed Actions
AI agents may:
- Explain loan pre-eligibility.
- Run a preliminary eligibility check using approved backend tools.
- Inform the customer whether they appear eligible for loan review.
- Explain that final approval requires underwriting.

Restricted Actions
AI agents must not:
- Approve loans.
- Promise loan disbursement.
- Guarantee interest rates.
- Confirm final eligibility.
- Override underwriting decisions.

Recommended Customer Response
If the customer passes pre-check, say that the customer appears pre-eligible for loan review and final approval requires underwriting, document verification, and credit policy checks.

If the customer does not pass pre-check, explain that current profile data does not meet pre-eligibility criteria and suggest contacting a loan officer for detailed review.

Escalation Rules
Escalate to lending operations if:
- Customer requests a high loan amount.
- Customer has high risk segment.
- Credit score is below threshold.
- Salary data is missing.
- Customer asks for final approval.

Sample Response
"Based on preliminary checks, you appear eligible for loan review. This is not final approval. Final approval requires underwriting and document verification."
