Document Title: Credit Card Late Fee Policy
Document Type: Banking Policy
Department: Cards Operations
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-07-01

Purpose
This document defines how credit card late fees are applied, reviewed, and escalated for customer support and banking operations.

Policy Overview
A credit card late fee is charged when a customer does not pay the minimum due amount before the payment due date. The fee may appear as "Credit Card Late Fee", "Late Payment Fee", or a similar description in the transaction history.

Late Fee Review Eligibility
A late fee review case can be created when all of the following conditions are met:
- The customer KYC status is verified.
- The customer risk segment is low or medium.
- The late fee amount is less than or equal to 1000.
- The customer does not have repeated late fee history in the recent review period.
- The customer has a valid explanation such as delayed salary, payment processing delay, or first-time missed payment.

Agent Allowed Actions
AI agents and customer support agents may:
- Explain why a late fee was charged.
- Search the customer's transactions to identify the late fee transaction.
- Create a fee review case if the customer requests review.
- Inform the customer that the operations team will review the case.

Restricted Actions
AI agents must not:
- Directly waive the late fee.
- Promise that the late fee will be reversed.
- Say that money has been credited back unless confirmed by an approved backend system.
- Modify account balances.

Recommended Customer Response
If a customer asks why a late fee was charged, explain that the fee is generally applied when the minimum due amount is not paid before the due date. If the customer requests a waiver, create a fee review case and clearly state that approval depends on operations review.

Escalation Rules
Escalate to human review if:
- The customer disputes the fee.
- The customer asks for refund or waiver.
- The fee amount is above 1000.
- Customer risk segment is high.
- Customer has repeated late fee history.

Sample Case Summary
"Customer requested review of credit card late fee transaction. Customer asked whether the fee can be reversed. Case created for operations team review."
