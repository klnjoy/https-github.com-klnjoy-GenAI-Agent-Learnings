Document Title: Refund and Reversal Policy
Document Type: Banking Policy
Department: Banking Operations
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-07-01

Purpose
This document defines refund and reversal restrictions for AI agents, customer support agents, and automated systems.

Policy Overview
Refund approval is a restricted banking action. Refunds, fee reversals, chargebacks, and account credits must be approved only by authorized banking operations teams or approved automated backend systems.

Agent Allowed Actions
AI agents may:
- Explain that refund requests require review.
- Search transactions related to the refund request.
- Retrieve applicable policy information.
- Create a human review case.
- Provide the customer with a case ID and next steps.

Restricted Actions
AI agents must not:
- Approve a refund.
- Promise a refund.
- Say "your money will be credited".
- Say "the fee has been waived".
- Say "refund has been processed" unless a backend system explicitly confirms it.
- Change account balance.
- Update transaction status.
- Override banking policy.

Refund Review Case Criteria
A refund review case may be created when:
- The customer requests money back.
- The customer requests late fee reversal.
- The customer reports a failed transaction.
- The customer reports duplicate charge.
- The customer disputes merchant billing.
- The customer requests exception handling.

Correct Agent Language
Use safe wording:
- "I have created a review case."
- "The operations team will review your request."
- "Final approval depends on policy and backend verification."
- "I cannot directly approve or process the refund."

Incorrect Agent Language
Do not use:
- "Your refund is approved."
- "The amount will be credited today."
- "I have waived your fee."
- "The transaction has been reversed."

Escalation Rules
Escalate refund requests to human review when:
- Customer asks for immediate refund.
- Customer claims financial loss.
- Customer threatens complaint.
- Transaction is high value.
- Policy eligibility is unclear.

Sample Case Summary
"Customer requested refund or reversal. AI agent cannot approve refund. Human review case created for operations team validation."
